A prayer-times app made as sadaqah jariyah

Azaan shows accurate prayer times for wherever you are, plays the adhan as recorded at the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, points you to the Qibla, and keeps the Hijri date. It is free, it has no adverts, no account and no tracking, and it never will.

  • Prayer times from the sun's position, with the calculation method your mosque follows and per-prayer adjustments to match a printed timetable
  • Load your mosque's own timetable file and follow it exactly
  • Adhan, chime, silent or off, for each prayer; quiet reminders before; Jumu'ah on Friday
  • Qibla compass and map
  • Ramadan: Sehri and Iftar countdown and a printable Ramadan calendar
  • Printable A4 timetable with your mosque's name, to share or print
  • Islamic calendar with reminders, prayer tracker, tasbih counter, widgets

Make your own mosque calendar

Any mosque or community can open Azaan, type its name and address, adjust the times to its own timetable, and share or print a clean A4 calendar for the month or for Ramadan. Share it freely.
