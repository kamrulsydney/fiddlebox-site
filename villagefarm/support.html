Common questions

Will my crops die if I don't play? No. Crops wait for you, fully grown, for as long as you like.

How do I get more land? Shop › Land grows your farm one step at a time as you level up. At level 10 you can buy a second farm.

How do I move things? Press and hold anything on your farm until it lifts, then drag it. Drop it on something the same size to swap them.

Where is the first-time guide? Open the Guide and tap “Show the first-time guide again”.

Is the farming information real? Yes. Each crop's Real Life card follows Bangladeshi farming and is checked by an agricultural expert before release. For your own land, ask your Upazila Agriculture Officer or call the Krishi Call Centre on 16123.
