A farming game rooted in real Bangladesh

Plant Boro, Aus and Aman rice in their real seasons, grow vegetables, spices and the flowers of Jashore, raise hens, ducks and cows, and turn your harvest into muri, chira, gur and more in village workshops. Ship produce from the ghat to real markets abroad. Every crop comes with a short Real Life card, checked by an agricultural expert before release.

  • Six Bengali seasons; each crop has its good season, like on a real farm
  • A square farm that grows in steps, from 5 × 5 to 32 × 32, and up to six farms: river char, haor, hill garden, coast and Barind
  • Remove the aail (ridges) between plots and farm the land as one
  • Decorate freely: paths, hedges, canals, trees, festival decorations for Pohela Boishakh, Eid and Puja
  • Village neighbours to help, trade with and receive gifts from
  • No crop ever withers and nothing forces you to pay
