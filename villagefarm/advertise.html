Your business on a plot beside the farm

Every farm in Village Farm is surrounded by the village: neighbours' farms, empty lots and a few sponsor plots. A sponsor plot shows your picture or logo with your name on an orange “Ad” sign. When a player taps it, the game asks if they want to visit, then opens your website.

  • Seen every time a player opens their farm: it stays there, it is not a video that interrupts play
  • Players are people who care about farming: farmers, families in villages and Bangladeshis abroad
  • At most 3 sponsor plots round each farm, all the same size, so your picture is never lost among adverts
  • Clearly labelled as an advert. No tracking: the game never collects anything about players for advertisers
  • Your picture can change any time, without waiting for an app update

What we need from you

  • Your business name, as it should appear on the sign

  • One short line about you, for example “Quality poultry feed since 1995”

Welcome

Seed and nursery companies, poultry, dairy, fish and cattle feed, fertiliser and farm machinery suppliers, agro shops and dealers, cold storage, agri apps and online shops, agricultural training and banks or mobile money with services for farmers.

Not accepted

Gambling, alcohol and tobacco, political adverts, and claims we cannot stand behind (for example, promises about fertiliser or pesticide results). We can remove any advert that breaks these rules.
