Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers Village Farm on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android (Google Play).

The short version

Village Farm has no account and no sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email address, location or contacts. Your farm is saved only on your device. We do not use analytics or tracking.

What the game downloads

When it opens, the game downloads one public file from this website (the list of sponsor plots and their pictures). Like any web page, the web host (GitHub Pages) sees the device's internet address to send the file. The game sends nothing else and nothing about you.

Sponsor plots (adverts)

Some plots beside the farm show a business's picture, labelled “Ad”. Nothing about you is shared with sponsors, and the game does not track what you tap. If you choose to visit a sponsor's website, it opens in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Optional purchases (in-game cash) are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game reminds you when crops are ready. These reminders are scheduled on your device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: kamrulsydney@yahoo.com.
