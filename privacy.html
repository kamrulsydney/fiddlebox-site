Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

The short version

Fiddlebox has no accounts, no sign-in and no profiles. We do not ask you for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and we never see your payment details. Your game progress stays on your device. The one thing that leaves your phone is advertising data, handled by Google, and only in the way described below.

What stays on your device

Your scores, your coins, which levels you have finished, and your settings are stored on your device using Apple's standard storage. We cannot read it, it is not uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Fiddlebox shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • general device information such as model, operating system version, and language
  • rough location derived from your IP address, at a country or city level
  • how adverts performed, for example whether one was shown, tapped or watched to the end

Google uses this to choose which adverts to show and to detect fraud. What Google does with it is covered by Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices. After you finish your first game, the app asks whether it may use your advertising identifier for personalised adverts. If you say no, you still see adverts, but they are not personalised. You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking, where you can also reset your advertising identifier. Where the law requires a consent form, such as in the United Kingdom, Google's consent form is shown before any advert loads, and you can reopen it from Settings inside the app by tapping Ad choices.

The four Easy games, the ones simple enough for young children, show no adverts.

Purchases

This version of Fiddlebox has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought. If purchases are added in a later version, they will go through Apple's In-App Purchase system, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

Fiddlebox is a general audience app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Some games in it are simple enough for young children; those Easy games show no adverts, and we would encourage a parent or guardian to decide whether the app suits their child. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

Analytics

We do not use any separate analytics service. We do not track individual players, build profiles, or share data with anyone besides the advertising provider named above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights over data held about you, including under the Australian Privacy Act, the European General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR, and California privacy law. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google using the link above. If you contact us we will help where we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the current version always lives at this address.
