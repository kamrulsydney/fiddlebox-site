What it is

Fiddlebox is a free box of small games for iPhone and iPad. Clear lines in the block game, then dip into Sudoku, Solitaire, Nonogram, Minefield, 2048, Memory, Word Hunt and more. Several games are built for two people sharing one phone.

No accounts. No forced adverts. Plays offline.
