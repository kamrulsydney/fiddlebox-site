Support

Fiddlebox is a box of small games: a block and line game, Sudoku, Solitaire, Nonogram, Minefield, 2048, Memory, Word Hunt, Slide 15, and games for two players on one phone.

Do I have to watch adverts?

No. Every advert that gives you something, such as extra moves or an undo, is offered on a card with a clear "not now". You can also buy the advert-free upgrade, which removes the banner and the full-screen adverts for good.

I bought something and it did not arrive

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Reinstalling over the top keeps it; uninstalling first does not.

Can I turn the sounds or the voice off?

Yes, both, in Settings on the main screen. The app also respects your phone's silent switch and the system Reduce Motion setting.

A game is too hard or too easy

Tell us which one and what happened. Difficulty is something we tune based on what people say.
